# Joker Reclaimed 1.0.0

Tear unwanted Jokers into permanent scraps and restore the unlocked Joker you want, with your choice of stickers and editions. First public release for Steamodded.

필요 없는 조커를 찢어 영구 조각을 모으고 원하는 조커를 복원하는 모드의 첫 공개 버전입니다. 치트 옵션은 제외했습니다.

- Persistent scraps across runs and profiles; tearing gives no sale money or sale effects.
- Unlocked-Joker catalogue, rarity filters, centered native previews and the F7 shop shortcut.
- Rental/Perishable replace the dollar price with $1, Eternal with $2; the base scrap cost stays unchanged.
- Foil/Holographic/Polychrome/Negative add 5/10/20/50 scraps. Eternal Jokers cannot be torn.
- All 15 Balatro languages, with introductions of at most 100 characters and usage text of at most 200 characters.
- 75 actual game screenshots and 15 illustrated guides, including each language's introduction screen.

## Downloads

- **JokerReclaimed-1.0.0.zip** — the installable mod, with localized text instructions. Extract `JokerReclaimed` into the Balatro Mods folder and restart the game.
- **JokerReclaimed-1.0.0-guides.zip** — all localized documents, original screenshots and guide images; optional, no installation required.
- Matching **.sha256** files verify each archive.

Requires Lovely and Steamodded, installed separately. Developed with Balatro 1.0.1o / Steamodded 26.829.0 / Lovely 0.10.0. Windows Mods path: `%APPDATA%\Balatro\Mods\`.
